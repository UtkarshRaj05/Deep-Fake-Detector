How this works

1

Locate the face

The system detects the largest face in the frame and crops in with margin, since manipulation artifacts concentrate around facial features.

2

Classify the texture

A convolutional network trained on real and manipulated faces scores the crop for the subtle blending seams, warping, and noise inconsistencies that betray synthetic content.

3

Show the evidence

An attention map (Grad-CAM) highlights exactly which regions drove the verdict, so the score isn't a black box.

No detector is perfect, and confident-looking output can still be wrong — treat this as one input among several when the stakes are high, not a final verdict.
